# UDD signal-integrity experiment contract

Date: 2026-09-15
State: DRAFT / reality branch

## Intent
Turn one real system-error pattern into a short UDD-format test without exposing private source material.

## Episode question
What happens when an AI system reasons over a wrong summary of an otherwise available source?

## Minimum structure
1. show a synthetic/non-personal source;
2. produce one deliberately lossy or incorrect summary;
3. give independent reviewers either source, summary, or both;
4. measure how often the wrong summary propagates;
5. allow `NULL` rather than forced classification;
6. keep source, derived claims, and corrections separately attributable.
